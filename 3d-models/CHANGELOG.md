### 3D Models Changelog

All notable changes, model fixes, and revisions for the printable 3D models are documented in this file.

### 2026-11-15 - Beta 2
- **Fixed**: Length of gt2-15t and gt2-16t parts.
- **Fixed**: Height of m3-damper.
- **Feature**: Power supply cover for safety.
- **Feature**: Changed base to use adapter for mainboard mounting. This allows using boards other than Manta M5P. Two new parts: manta-m5p-adapter-left and manta-m5p-adapter-right.
- **Feature**: Two new parts for warp-correction of the base corners: warp-correction-1 and warp-correction-2 (3 for front and side corners and one for back). 
- **Chore**: Removed technical text imprint on gantry-xy-lr-0.

### 2026-11-01
- **Fixed**: `timing-belt-holder-bottom.3mf` broken model issue in Beta 1.

### 2026-10-27 - Beta 1
